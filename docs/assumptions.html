Modeling assumptions

  • Inspections and education visits change retailer behavior; the size of the effect is assumed and flagged as such in the write-up.
  • The DOH is the client, scoped to Pittsburgh (real-world ownership is state-level; Pittsburgh is a test case).
  • Predict-then-optimize: decisions do not change the risk model’s inputs within a horizon (except via the explicit Harrington state), so ML is not embedded in the MILP (ADR 0003).
